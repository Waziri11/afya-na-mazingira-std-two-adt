Imba shairi lifuatalo, kisha jibu swali.
Shairi
Utembeapo jamani, zingatia usalama,
Uwapo barabarani, tambua hizo alama,
Nyumbani au shuleni, sote tufike salama,
Usalama zingatia, uwapo barabarani.
Utembelee kulia, ndiko kwenye usalama,
Kwenye kivuko vukia, kwanza kulia tazama,
Kushoto we angalia, kulia tena tazama,
Usalama zingatia, uwapo barabarani.
Magari yakisimama, hapo kuna usalama,
Waweza vuka salama, na wala hautakwama,
Haya uliyoyasoma, yazingatie daima,
Usalama zingatia, uwapo barabarani.
